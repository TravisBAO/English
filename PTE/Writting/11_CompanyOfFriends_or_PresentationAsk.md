
You're going to give a presentation in the company of your friend Jim, but you are not familiar with the venue there, so you are writing to ask about the basic information of the company. You should write at least 100 words. Your ideas must come from the following three themes:

-Describe your situation, including specific time;
-Ask about the availability of technology equipment such as computers and projectors;
-Ask about the number of participants.


## 我最终背诵的版本 （字有点多，不用死记硬背，只要没语法错误就行

Dear Jim,

I am writing to confirm a few venue details regarding/for my upcoming presentation at your company on Friday, October 20th, at 10:00 AM.

Could you please confirm the availability of technology equipment in the room, such as computers, projectors, and audio systems? If I am required to bring anything, please inform me in advance.

Additionally, I would appreciate it if you could let me know how many participants are expected to attend the session. Knowing the precise/exact number of participants will help me adjust/tailor/customize the presentation slides/materials and prepare enough printed copies/documents/materials. Please let me know if there are any specific guidelines I should follow.

Thank you very much for your assistance, and I look forward to hearing from you soon.

Best regards,
Travis


## 另外的写法
第一段
I am writing to confirm a few venue details regarding my upcoming presentation at your company. As a reminder, the event is scheduled on Friday, October, at 10:00 PM.

第二段
Please let me know in advance if I need to bring any of my own equipment.



Notes:
 - 绝对不能用复数！equipment 是不可数名词（Uncountable Noun），永远不能加 -s。即使你想表达“很多设备”或“各种设备”，也不能写成 equipments。
 - 在英语写作（尤其是标准职场邮件和 PTE 官方推荐格式）中，先写星期和日期，再写具体时间点是最自然、最地道的顺序。
