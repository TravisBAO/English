Subject: Invitation: Alex’s Birthday Celebration!

Dear Parents,

I hope this message finds you well. As next month **marks my son Alex’s birthday**, I am delighted to invite your child to join his celebration.

The party will be held on Saturday, June 15th, from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM at Central Park’s **main pavilion**. We have planned a variety of **engaging activities**, including a magic show and several outdoor games to keep the children entertained. Regarding refreshments, a selection of snacks and a birthday cake will be provided. Please let me know if your child has any specific **dietary requirements** so we can accommodate them.

We would be thrilled to have you celebrate with us. Please RSVP by next Friday.

Best regards,

Travis

## 高级表达 (Lexical Sophistication):

 - 用 "marks my son's birthday" 代替 is my son's birthday。

 - 用 "engaging activities" (引人入胜的活动) 代替 fun things to do。

 - 用 "dietary requirements" (饮食要求) 代替 food allergies，这在正式邀请中非常地道且专业。

## 句式衔接 (Cohesion):

 - 使用 "Regarding refreshments..." 来引出食物的话题，这种“话题引导词”能显著提升你的逻辑分。

 - 使用了 "so we can accommodate them" 的目的状语从句，展现了复杂句式的运用。

## 地道习惯 (Native Nuance):

 - RSVP: 这是国际通用的“请回复”缩写，在邀请类邮件中是加分项。
