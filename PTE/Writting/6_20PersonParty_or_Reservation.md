Dear Restaurant Manager,

I am writing to inquire about reserving a large table at Oscar's Kitchen. I tried booking online for next Friday, September 11th, at 7:00 PM, but the website only allows reservations for up to six people.

We are planning a birthday party for my close friend, expecting around twenty guests. During the celebration, we intend to enjoy a group dinner, share a birthday cake, and toast to the special occasion. Additionally, could you please let me know if there are dedicated parking spaces available in the building for our guests?

I look forward to hearing from you to confirm this reservation.

Best regards,
Travis
