Subject: Proposals for the upcoming community Easter event promotion

Dear Alex,

I am writing to propose several strategies to effectively promote our upcoming Easter event.

First and foremost, I suggest organizing engaging activities, such as a neighborhood egg hunt, to attract families and foster community spirit. Regarding digital outreach, leveraging social media platforms is essential. By creating interactive posts and event pages, we can significantly increase our visibility and reach a wider audience. Lastly, collaborating with local businesses would be highly beneficial. We could partner with nearby cafes or shops for sponsorships, which would provide mutual support and strengthen local ties.

I believe these initiatives will ensure the event's success. I look forward to discussing these ideas further with you.

Best regards,

Travis

## 高级职场词汇 (Professional Vocabulary):

 - 用 "leverage" (杠杆/利用) 代替 use。

 - 用 "initiatives" (倡议/新举措) 代替 ideas 或 plans。

 - 用 "mutual support" (互助/互惠) 描述与当地商家的关系，非常地道。

## 严谨的逻辑支撑 (Supporting Ideas):

 - 题目要求提供 "supporting ideas"。范文中不仅提到了活动，还解释了为什么要办（为了吸引家庭、增加曝光、加强联系），这种“观点+解释”的结构是高分的关键。

## 连接词的精准切换 (Cohesive Devices):

 - 使用了 "Regarding digital outreach"（关于数字推广）作为过渡，比起只会用 Secondly 的考生，这展示了更高的语言驾驭能力。
