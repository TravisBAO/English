Write an email to your neighbor who has just moved in and ask him/her to address some issues. You should write between 80 and 120 words.

The issues mentioned in the email should include the following three words:

- Noise;
- Parking;
- Garbage.

## 我最终背诵的版本 （118）

Dear John,

Welcome to the neighborhood! I am writing this email to politely address a few small issues regarding your recent move-in, so we can maintain a peaceful living environment.

First, could you please keep the noise down during late hours, as sound easily passes through the walls? Second, regarding parking, kindly ensure your vehicles are parked strictly within your assigned spot to keep the common driveway clear for everyone. Lastly, please place your household garbage inside the designated bins according to the local collection schedule rather than leaving bags outside. Please feel free to reach out if you ever need any help settling in.

Thank you very much for your understanding and quick assistance.

Best regards,
Travis
