Dear Marvin,

I am writing to formally **request your assistance in** resolving several persistent issues within my apartment. Although I have only **been renting** here for a month, my **living experience** has been significantly impacted.

Firstly, the constant noise originating from the neighbor above has severely disrupted my sleep. I would appreciate it if you could **liaise with them** to ensure quiet hours are respected. Furthermore, the hot water system **has been malfunctioning repeatedly**. As this is a **recurring problem**, I strongly recommend **dispatching** a **qualified technician** to perform a **thorough/comprehensive/complete repair**.

I would greatly appreciate your prompt attention to these matters. Please let me know if you require further details. Thank you for your time.

Best regards,

Travis


# 💡 核心提分点解析 (CLB 10 级准则)
## 精准的动词 (Action Verbs):

 - Liaise with them: 替代 tell him or her。在处理邻里纠纷时，物业经理的作用是“联络/协调”，这个词非常高级。

 - Malfunctioning: 替代 doesn't work well。这是描述设备故障最专业、最地道的词汇。

 - Dispatching: 替代 ask。在商务语境下，“派遣”技术人员用 dispatch。

## 句式结构 (Grammar):

 - Although I have only been renting...: 使用让步状语从句，展示了你能平衡复杂信息的能力。

 - Severely disrupted: 副词 + 动词的组合，加强了情感表达的力度。

 - Request assistance in [doing]...: 修正了原文的 request for your help。注意：request 作为动词时直接接宾语。

## 地道术语 (Terminology):

 - Recurring problem: 替代 not the first time。表示“反复出现的问题”。

 - Qualified technician: 替代 technician，增加了对专业程度的要求，显得你说话更有分量。
