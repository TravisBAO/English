Write an email to your neighbor who has just moved in and ask him/her to address some issues. You should write between 80 and 120 words.

The issues mentioned in the email should include the following three words:

- Noise;
- Parking;
- Garbage.

## 我最终背诵的版本 （118）

Dear John,

Welcome to the community! I am writing this email to politely address a few small issues regarding your recent relocation/arrival/move-in, so we can maintain a peaceful living environment.

First, could you please keep the noise down late at night/during late hours, as sound easily travels/passes through the walls? Second, regarding parking, kindly ensure your vehicle is parked strictly within your assigned spot to keep the common driveway clear for others. Lastly, please place your household waste inside the proper/designated bins according to the local collection schedule, rather than leaving bags outside. 

Please feel free to reach out if you ever need any help settling in/any help with your relocation.
Thank you very much for your understanding and cooperation/quick assistance.

Best regards,
Travis
