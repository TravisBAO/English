Subject: Letter of Recommendation – Travis

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to highly recommend my former colleague, Travis, for a position within your organization. Having worked closely with him for three years, I can vouch for his exceptional professional character.

Travis possesses an admirable personality, characterized by a positive attitude and a collaborative spirit. Regarding his abilities, he demonstrates remarkable analytical skills and the capacity to handle complex tasks under pressure. Furthermore, he made significant contributions to our team, most notably by streamlining our project management workflow, which increased overall efficiency by 15%.

I am confident that Travis would be a valuable asset to your company. Please feel free to contact me for any further information.

Best regards,

Jennifer


💡 核心高分表达解析
## 职业化动词 (Professional Verbs):

 - Vouch for: 这是一个非常高级的词，意为“为……担保/作证”。

 - Streamlining: 意为“精简/优化（流程）”，是职场描述成就时的黄金词汇。

 - Possesses: 比用 has 显得更加正式地道。

## 特质描述 (Personality & Abilities):

 - Collaborative spirit: 合作精神。

 - Remarkable analytical skills: 出众的分析能力。

 - Capacity to handle pressure: 顶住压力的能力。

## 量化贡献 (Quantifying Contributions):

 - 在推荐信中，提到具体的百分比（如 increased efficiency by 15%）会极大地增强可信度，也是高分作文的典型特征。
