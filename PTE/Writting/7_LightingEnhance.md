Dear Apartment Manager,

I am writing to formally **request an improvement to** the lighting within our building. **Currently**, there is only a single dim light bulb at the entrance, and the stairwell remains entirely **unlit**. Consequently, I feel quite anxious when navigating the hallway and stairs at night.

**To address these safety concerns**, I **suggest installing** additional fixtures along the stairwell and replacing the existing entrance light with a brighter alternative. Furthermore, I believe that motion-sensor lights **would be an ideal solution**, as they provide necessary **illumination** while effectively **conserving energy**.

I would greatly appreciate your prompt attention to this matter. Please contact me if you require further details. Thank you for your time.

Best regards,

Travis



# 💡 核心修改说明
## 词汇精准度 (Precision):

 - Dim light bulb: 替代 weak light buld，"dim" 描述灯光微弱更专业。

 - Anxious: 替代 nervous，在描述安全顾虑时更有分量。

 - Fixtures: 替代简单的 lights，指代照明固定装置，展现职场词汇量。

 - Conserving energy: 替代 save the energy，这是环境话题中的高分固定搭配。

## 语法纠错 (Grammar):

 - Request to enhance / Request for an improvement: Request 作为动词时后面直接加宾语，作为名词时后面接 for。

 - Entirely unlit: 替代 no other lights，用副词+形容词的结构更具文学性和逻辑性。

## 句式衔接 (Cohesion):

 - 使用了 "To address these safety concerns..." 开头，明确了建议的目的，逻辑性极强。
