Your company is trying to save energy, and you want to write an e-mail to make some suggestions to the manager, Mrs. Smith about energy reduction. You should write at least 100 words. Your ideas must come from the following three themes:
-Turing off the lights.
-Shutting down computers.
-Heating and air-conditioning.

## 我最终背诵的版本 （112）
Dear Mrs. Smith,

I am writing to share a few practical suggestions regarding our company's initiative to reduce energy consumption in the office.

To help lower our energy usage, I would like to propose three main measures.

First, we should encourage staff to form a habit of turning off the lights when leaving empty meeting rooms or office areas. Second, employees should be reminded about shutting down computers completely at the end of each workday rather than leaving them in standby mode. Finally, optimizing the settings for heating and air-conditioning by adjusting thermostats by just a few degrees could significantly cut electricity costs.

Thank you for considering these ideas.

Best regards,
Travis

全面覆盖三大主题 (Three Themes Covered)：

Turning off the lights: turning off the lights when leaving empty meeting rooms（离开空会议室时关灯）

Shutting down computers: shutting down computers completely at the end of each workday（下班时彻底关闭电脑）

Heating and air-conditioning: optimizing the settings for heating and air-conditioning（优化暖气和空调的设定温度）
