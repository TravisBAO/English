


Subject: Group reservation inquiry for a birthday celebration – Next Friday

Dear Restaurant Manager,

I am writing to **formally inquire** about a reservation for a birthday party for twenty guests next Friday. **Although** I attempted to book online, I noticed that the website limits reservations to a maximum of six people.

During the celebration, we **intend** to present a cake and sing for the **birthday person**. Could you please confirm if your establishment can **accommodate** a group of this size? Additionally, as your restaurant is located within a large building, I am wondering if there are **dedicated parking facilities** available for our guests.

I would greatly appreciate your assistance in securing this booking. I look forward to your prompt response.

Best regards,

Travis



# 核心提分修改解析

## 地道词汇替换 (Critical Fixes):

 - 寿星 (Shouxing): 英语对应 birthday person

 - Accommodate: 这是一个高级动词，表示“容纳/提供空间”，比用 have a larger table 显得专业得多。

 - Establishment: 再次推荐这个词，用来替代重复出现的 restaurant。

## 语法与句式 (Grammar):

 - I noticed... while...: 原文的句子结构略显破碎。修改版使用了 "Although... I noticed..." 这种让步状语从句，展示了更强的逻辑控制力。

 - Dedicated parking facilities: 替代基础的 parking space，词汇量加分。

## 语气 (Register):

 - 使用了 "formally inquire" 开头和 "greatly appreciate" 结尾，符合 CLB 9/10 对正式商务邮件的要求。
